I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.
